# I-788 · design review A · "more below" is a row of the footer, not a pill over the page

The owner approved proposal A of the phone design review (build f4f43d17). The mock-up is the
target: a slim row at the top of the footer, a 28 × 3 grab bar and the words "more below" in muted
12 px, centred; the buttons under it whole; the list scrolling behind the existing fade.

Session B's `b/I-788-A` was a head start only: it drew a 40 px bordered row _between_ the body and
the footer with a ▾ glyph. The mock-up puts the row _inside_ the footer with a grab bar and no
border, so the SDK `Screen` was rebuilt to the mock-up instead of cherry-picked:

- `Screen.tsx`: two states. `room` lays the row out while the body overflows by more than 24 px,
  measured as if the row were absent, so laying it out can never flip it back off; `more` only
  fades it (opacity + visibility) when the reader reaches the end, so nothing moves under a
  finger. With a footer the row is the footer's first child; with no footer the same row sits
  alone in a strip padded into the bottom safe area.
- `Screen.module.css`: `.cue` (full-width button, 28 px drawn; a `::after` reaches up to the
  footer's top edge for a 40 px thumb target and never down over the button), `.cueOnly` for the
  footer-less strip, a landscape variant, reduced-motion / motion-off without the fade. The
  I-066 B pill and I-187's zero-height anchor are gone.
- Strings: `'more below'` → `'hay más abajo'` (the aria-label stays "scroll down" / "ver más abajo").
- `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` updated (the ▾ pill described twice).
