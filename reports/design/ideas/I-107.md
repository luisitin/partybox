# I-107 · The verdict's icon lights the line that actually won (option B)

Branch `b/I-107-B` cherry-picked clean (`Card.tsx`, `Card.module.css`, `Tv.tsx`).

## What ships

- **A** — the verdict's `PatternIcon` gets `view.claim.cells` — the completion `evaluate()` chose —
  instead of the pattern's example cells, so a diagonal win shows the diagonal and an O-column win
  shows the O column.
- **B** — those cells draw in one after another, 70 ms apart (opacity only, 200 ms each), on the
  same beat as the card's sweep.

Two repo rules the branch predates: `Tv.tsx` was one line over the 300-line cap (`phaseId` folded
into the `view` destructure), and the new animation needed its `prefers-reduced-motion` and
`data-motion='off'` twins, which were added.

## Proof

Scratchpad `c-capture/probe_I107.ts` on the real verdict stage (`/preview/bingo/bingo?view=tv`),
whose fixture claim won on the **middle column** (cells 2, 7, 12, 17, 22) while the pattern's
example is a row:

```

[{"lit":[2,7,12,17,22],"delays":["0s","0.07s","0.14s","0.21s","0.28s"],"anim":"pb-icon-draw"}]
```

The icon lights the column the card actually won on, and its five cells come in 70 ms apart.
