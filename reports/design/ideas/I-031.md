# I-031 · Character select, with a photo avatar — option B + the owner's note (shipped)

Spec: `C:\dev\partybox-ideas\specs\I-031.md` (the owner's pick, branch `b/I-031-B`, with the note
"an option on the top in the portrait area to upload your own photo from your phone").

- The branch's A + B as it has them (the pop, the 55 % step-back, the 96 px portrait row).
- The note: `JoinPortrait.tsx` adds "Use a photo" beside the portrait (a file input with
  `accept="image/*" capture="user"`), `photo.ts` makes the 128 × 128 JPEG on the phone (centre
  crop, quality 0.8 → 0.6 → 0.4 under the 24 KB cap, EXIF stripped by the re-encode, orientation
  honoured by `createImageBitmap`), "Use a face instead" reverts, the grid steps back while a
  photo is up. The phone keeps the photo with its name and face.
- Contract (ADR-037, not the spec's `ViewPlayer.photo`): a 24 KB picture in every view push at
  sixteen players is too heavy, so the snapshot alone carries `photo` and a photo player's avatar
  id is `photo:<id>` wherever an avatar id flows; `Avatar` resolves it through the shell's
  `AvatarPhotos` context, with a `photo` prop for the form's own preview. Thirty `<Avatar>` sites
  — chips, scoreboards, rosters, voters, readers, seats, winners — show the photo untouched.
  `photoSchema` and `avatarIdOf` are unit-tested.
