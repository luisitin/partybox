# I-079 · A seasonal face in the join grid (option C)

Branch `b/I-079-C` reapplied (only `Join.tsx` / `Join.module.css` conflicted, against I-031's photo
avatar, I-059's preview stage and I-076's language row).

## What ships

- **A** — `pumpkin` (October), `snowflake` (December) and `heart` (February) are real avatar ids
  **every** day, so a chip picked in October never breaks in November. `?date=YYYY-MM-DD` on the
  join link previews a month.
- **B** — in season the seasonal face takes the unicorn's slot: sixteen cells stay sixteen, and the
  unicorn is back next month. The random default face still comes from the everyday sixteen.
- **C** — the seasonal cell carries a small "THIS MONTH" tag (accent pill, `aria-hidden`; the face
  itself is the signal, so colour is never the only one).

`Join.tsx` was over the 300-line cap, so the URL readers moved to `controller/joinUrl.ts`
(`roomFromUrl`, `previewDate`, `joinGrid`) and the I-076 language row to `controller/JoinLangs.tsx`.
