# I-648 option B: guests see the settings while the VIP tunes, and a change breathes

Shipped from `b/I-648-B` (cherry-picked: `SettingsGlance.tsx`, its CSS, `settingValue` in
`tunedLine.ts`, `settings-glance.test.ts`, the `Selecting.tsx` mount). The owner's binding note,
"make the glow look smoother (like a breathe)", replaced the branch's hard cut (the fill snapped on
with a 0.3 s fade and snapped off at 2 s) with one breath.

## What changed on top of the branch

- **The breath.** A changed row's yellow fill and its dark ink swell in and ease out again over
  2 s (`--pb-motion-pulse` × 2, `ease-in-out`, the curve the shell's `pb-breathe` already uses).
  Colour only; nothing moves. Reduced motion (the OS setting or the phone's motion switch) holds the
  fill flat for the same 2 s instead: the tokens collapse to 0 ms and a zero-length breath would
  otherwise show nothing.
- **A second change restarts the breath.** The lit row remounts under a new key, so Rounds 9 and
  then Rounds 10 0.9 s later gives a second full breath rather than a half one.
- **The glow no longer sticks.** On the branch, any room snapshot inside the 2 s (a vote, a player
  joining) re-ran the effect and cleared the timer that ends the glow, so the row stayed lit. The
  off timer now lives in its own effect, keyed on the change.
- **A language switch does not light every row.** Its new words are a translation, not a change.
- **200 % text.** The branch's `min-width: 0` + `nowrap` let "WILD (all three decks)" run over its
  "Decks" label on the Pixel at 200 % text. Both sides now keep their min-content width, and the
  value wraps onto a second line.
