# I-148 · Blanks · your hand while the judge picks — shipped option C

Branch `b/I-148-C` applied: the controller view sends `hand` during `pick` too (A), a tap on a
candidate reads the phone's front card into its blank (B), the hand strip comes out of its 55 %
dim as the judge takes one (C).

Merge and fixes:

- `views.ts` conflict: main had since added the I-147 tie-break rule (`!sitsOut`); both kept — the
  hand travels in `answer` or `pick`, never to the judge, never to a player sitting out a tie-break.
- `ControllerPick` moved to its own file (`ControllerPick.tsx`): with the option `ControllerHand.tsx`
  ran past the 300-line lint ceiling.
- **The hand strip hung off both edges of the phone.** The waiting screen centres its children, so
  the branch's unsized row grew to all ten cards and was centred: on an iPhone SE the first card
  sat half off the left edge, out of reach of the scroll. The row now takes the questions' width
  (`width: 100%; max-width: 34rem`), cards do not shrink, text starts left.
- The two new screen-reader labels go through `L()` ("your hand" already had Spanish; "try your
  first card in this question" → "prueba tu primera carta en esta pregunta"). The CSS comments'
  literal `\u2500` escapes became the characters.
- New test (`pick.test.ts`): during `pick` a non-judge's view carries their whole hand, the judge's
  is empty, and a play is still refused.
