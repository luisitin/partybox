# I-087 · Every avatar blinks (option C)

Branch `b/I-087-C` cherry-picked clean — `styles/global.css` and `game-sdk/ui/Avatar.tsx`. The
face art then moved to `game-sdk/ui/avatarArt.tsx` for the 300-line cap (the probe was re-run after
the split and reads the same).

## What ships

- **A** — `eyes()` now renders inside `<g class="pb-eyes">`; the stylesheet closes it for ~150 ms
  every 6 s (`scaleY`, transform only), with a per-face delay hashed from the avatar id so a roster
  never blinks in unison. No per-avatar JS; it applies everywhere an avatar renders. Robots draw
  their own eyes and never blink.
- **B** — odd-hashed faces run `pb-blink-twice`.
- **C** — a dimmed face (a dropped player, I-009) carries `data-asleep`: eyes shut, no blink, until
  they are back.

Reduced motion and the `data-motion='off'` switch both stop it.

## Proof

Scratchpad `c-capture/probe_I087.ts`, the real TV lobby with Sam (owl) and Priya (koala), reading
computed styles:

```
lobby faces     koala pb-blink 2.9s asleep=false | owl pb-blink 0.2s asleep=false
after a drop    koala none    0s   asleep=true  | owl pb-blink 0.2s asleep=false
after a return  koala pb-blink 2.9s asleep=false | owl pb-blink 0.2s asleep=false
motion off      koala none    0s               | owl none 0s
```

The two delays the spec recorded (koala 2.9 s, owl 0.2 s) — they never blink together — the dropped
face sleeping and waking, and the motion switch stopping both. `pnpm verify` GREEN.
