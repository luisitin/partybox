# I-789 B (design review) — all four answers fit, on every phone, and stay put after you tap

The owner approved proposal B of the design review (f4f43d17); its drawn mock-up is the target.
`b/I-789-B` was the head start (layout wrapper, 2×2, held status line); where it differed from the
mock-up the mock-up won.

## What ships (SDK `ChoiceGrid`, `fill` grids — Lightning's questions and wager)

- The question (kicker + prompt) sits over the answers; the answer tiles share the height left:
  66 px each when there is room, down to their own content on a short phone (one line: 44 px on
  an SE), never below it (a two-line answer is never clipped, the body scrolls instead of the tiles
  sliding under anything). They never stretch past 66 px (or two lines), so nothing drifts.
- Four short answers (≤ 12 characters) go two by two; the ✓/✗ sits in the tile's corner; a word is
  never split (it drops under the letter when it cannot sit beside it); at 200 % text the 2×2 falls
  back to one column (container query on the layout, 14 em).
- Sideways (landscape, ≤ 500 px tall): the question on the left, the answers on the right — a 2×2
  for short answers (the drawing), a column of four for long ones (half a sideways phone split
  their words).
- Locking in moves nothing: the "Locked in" line's space is held from the first frame (two lines on
  phones ≤ 440 px wide, where "✓ Locked in with 12 s to spare — look at the TV" wraps), and
  Lightning's "+N now" worth line keeps its height (hidden) after the tap. Your tile gets the ring,
  the others only dim.
- Short phones (≤ 600 px tall): the question steps down to body size, gaps tighten.
- The wager (I-550) is a fill grid too: it drops the held line (its presets never resize).
