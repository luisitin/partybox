- The blot is on every phone card, not only the Focus card: the branch's `.phone .cell` selector
  is the Focus / tablet card's size class; Grid, Stack, Side by side, Strip and the thumbnails are
  `.compact`. Both now carry the blot (`:is(.phone, .compact)`), as the spec's behaviour asks.
- The phone's own verdict card keeps its greens and reds: the branch's selector out-specified
  `.green` / `.red` on the wiped-claim card, so the verdict would have shown orange blots instead
  of the check's colours. Excluded with `:not(.free, .green, .red, .dim)`.
- The cell that completes the line keeps its stamp landing: `.lineHit` alone replaced the
  `stamp-land` animation on that cell (same property, later rule). `.stamp.lineHit` runs both,
  the bump after the landing. Reduced-motion / motion-off twins cover `.lineHit` too.
- `--pb-daub` is set on the existing in-round body `div` (Controller.tsx is at its 300-line cap),
  not on a new wrapper — same scope: every layout.
