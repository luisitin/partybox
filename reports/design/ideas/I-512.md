# I-512 B — the verdict lands a beat after the last guess, and Next waits for it

Shipped from `b/I-512-B` (commit `4a67164f`, cherry-picked clean onto `session-c` after I-211 B).

- **A** — on a book's last page the guess shows alone; the verdict box rises 2.5 s later (3.5 s for
  an UNBROKEN book). The verdict box and its `Beat` delay now live in `client/Verdict.tsx`, because
  `Tv.tsx` went over the 300-line lint limit with I-211 and I-512 together. The markup is unchanged.
- **B** — the server ignores the presenter's `turn` on a last page until the beat has passed. The
  TV's Skip / VIP skip and the fallback timer still turn the page.
- **Deviation (binding behaviour kept):** the branch held Next for a flat 2.5 s, but the TV shows
  an UNBROKEN verdict at 3.5 s. That left a 1 s window where a turn skipped a verdict nobody had
  seen, which breaks "can't turn past a last page until its verdict has shown". The server now
  holds an intact page for `VERDICT_BEAT_INTACT_MS` = 3 500 ms. A broken page still uses
  `VERDICT_BEAT_MS` = 2 500. `verdict-beat.test.ts` covers broken, intact and a mid-book page.
- No new view field, input, sound or string.
