# I-793 · join · design review F: joining fits on one screen — shipped (proposal F)

No Session B branch existed for this idea (`b/I-793-*` absent); built from the design review's drawn
mock-up (`specs/design-review-f4f43d17/phone-screens-redrawn.html#F`), which the owner approved.

- Header: the five language pills became one "🌐 EN ▾" (`JoinLangPick`: the phone's own list over
  the pill, languages in their own words). The 🎨 sheet keeps its pill row.
- Title "Join the party" over "Room XPVW · 5 people are in" (new `roomLabel` / `peopleIn` in all five
  join languages).
- The portrait (64 px) and the name field on one row; the portrait is the photo button (📷 badge; ✕
  with a photo up — "Use a face instead"). The big portrait name and its typing dots (I-060 B) are
  gone: the name is in the field right beside it.
- Faces: one sideways-swiping strip ("Face · swipe for more"), every face in the chosen colour, the
  last one fading at the edge; the picked face is scrolled into view when it starts off-strip.
- Colours: one row of eight dots under a "Colour" label. Join stays in the sticky footer.
- Sideways (I-067 B): the strip and dots are the right column, the strip no longer bleeds left.
