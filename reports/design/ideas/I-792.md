No Session B branch existed for this idea (`b/I-792-*` absent); built from the design review's drawn
mock-up (`specs/design-review-f4f43d17/phone-screens-redrawn.html#E`), which the owner approved.

- Title row: "Lobby 6 of 16" (the count gives way first), 🔗 Share, ⋯. The pills' type is capped
  (15 px) so the row stays one line at 200 % text.

  phone while you wait, 🚪 Leave (tap to arm, tap to go, as before).

  200 % text), `PlayerChip compact` (no "you" tag — your chip is ringed instead — ★ alone for the
  VIP, no empty glyph slot), then "＋ Add a bot".

  change never moves the screen): VIP — the vote tally, "Everyone's in? Pick a game. Bots can fill
  empty seats." and the first-room tips (✕ still retires them); guest — "Waiting for the VIP…" and
  the bots hint. The guest's vote row stays under it.
