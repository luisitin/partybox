# I-796 · design review K · "Look at the TV" screens give your phone something to hold

The owner approved proposal K of the phone design review (build f4f43d17). No Session B branch
existed; built to the mock-up (Wisecrack's reveal), with the proposal's Broken Pencil thumbnail.

## What changed

- **Wisecrack** `RevealMirror.tsx` (+ `.module.css`): in a TV room the reveal phone no longer shows
  a spinner and "Authors revealed! / Look at the TV" (non-voters), "You picked A / See who wrote it
  on the TV" (voters) or "Your answer is up / Look at the TV" (authors, until the hold). It shows
  "On the TV now · prompt 5 of 6", the prompt, both answers as mini cards (A / B, "yours" or "your
  pick" tagged), the author names at the TV's author beat (900 ms), votes and points at its last
  beat (1.8 s, the same hold an author's result waits for — the phone never spoils the TV), the
  winner's card outlined, and "You weren't in this one: 1 more to go." / "You picked A: …" / "Your
  answer is in this one: …" / "…: scores are next.". The author's own result screen still follows
  the hold. The server's controller `reveal` gained `number` and `count`. Phone-only rooms keep
  their full `PhoneReveal`.
- **Broken Pencil** `Show.tsx`: in a TV room the non-presenters see "{name} is presenting", the
  book/page kicker, a framed thumbnail of the page on the TV ("On the TV now"; a drawing capped at
  220 px, a word full width so it never breaks mid-word) and "Your turn comes when your book is
  up."; the presenter gets the same thumbnail above the turn button.
- Strings with Spanish in both games' tables; a README line in each.

## Proof (probe `mirror-796.tmp.ts`, port 42221; iPhone 15 VIP who never votes, iPhone SE, Pixel 7, Pixel 200 %)

Wisecrack, three reveals, each phone shot at 250 ms / 1.3 s / 2.6 s into the reveal:

- 250 ms: every non-author phone shows the mirror with letters only ("A your pick … B …") — no
  names, no votes. 1.3 s: "A · BOT 1 … B · KENJI". 2.6 s: "+350 · 3 votes" / "0 votes", winner
  outlined; when both score (+200 / +100) only the winner is outlined.
- VIP (never voted): "You weren't in this one: 5 more to go." — the review's drawn state. SE voter:
  "You picked A: 2 more to go."; last prompt: "You picked A: scores are next." Author (200 % text):
  mirror tagged "yours" until 1.8 s, then its own "+0 No votes this time" result.
- iPhone SE: the two cards side by side (as drawn); at 200 % text they stack and the footer's
  "more below" row appears. No horizontal overflow on any of 48 stills.

Broken Pencil `show`, three pages (a word, a drawing, a guess) × 4 phones: every phone carries the
thumbnail (`data-page-thumb`), iPhone 361 px wide, SE 288, drawing capped at 220 px; at 200 % the
word "washing a whale" no longer breaks mid-word; no horizontal overflow.
