# I-288 B — the phone's clock tells the truth (+ what an answer is worth now)

Shipped from `b/I-288-B` (cherry-picked; the two conflicts with I-589 — a view field and the Spanish
table — keep both sides).

## What ships

- **A** — Lightning's controller view sets `timerMode: 'quiet'` in `intro` and `reveal` (the TV's
  own rule), so the phone keeps the bar and drops the seconds when nothing can be pressed. The same
  one line in Wisecrack's controller view: digits in `answer` / `vote`, quiet elsewhere.
- **B** — while a regular question is open and not yet locked, the phone shows "+N now" under the
  question (`worth` on the controller view: base, speed max, streak bonus, window), draining ten
  times a second with the speed bonus. Not on the final question (the bet is the stake there).
  Spanish: "+N ahora".
- One deviation: the branch counted from `Date.now()`; shipped counts from `useServerNow(100)` (the
  shell's server-clock offset) so a phone whose clock is off never promises points the server will
  not give. Same cadence, same numbers on a synced phone.
