# I-791 · design review D · offline is a state you can see; coming back is one clean step

The owner approved proposal D of the phone design review (build f4f43d17). No Session B branch
existed for it; built to the mock-up.

## What changed

- `packages/client/src/controller/OfflineCard.tsx` (+ `.module.css`): `useLinkCard` keeps the
  owner's flap rule (1.2 s of trouble before anything shows; the link must hold 600 ms before the
  card may say "You're back") and snapshots the last view as the link goes. `OfflineCard`: a veil
  over the whole phone (takes every tap), spinner, "Reconnecting…", "Your phone lost the party's
  Wi‑Fi. Your seat is held for **1:57**." (the server's 120 s grace, I-089 C) and a pill — "✓ Your
  answer was sent" or "You haven't answered yet — you still can once you're back". `BackCard`: for
  1.1 s over the body (header and timer stay): ✓, "You're back", "You missed questions 1 and 2. /
  This is question 3 of 10: 8 seconds left.", and "You · 3rd · 1,000" from the view's scores.
- `ControllerShell`: the old 13 px "Reconnecting… 1:57 left" line (in the timer bar or the
  top banner) is replaced by the card; the game dims (opacity 0.5, saturate 0.3) and is `inert`
  while offline and during the back beat; the timer reads "—" offline.
- No ghost of the old screen: `Screen` and `CrossfadeSwap` tag their dissolve snapshots
  `data-pb-ghost`, and the shell hides them while `data-resync` is set (offline and back).
- Contract: optional `ViewEnvelope.progressStep { unit: 'question' | 'round', n, of }`; Lightning
  stamps it on regular questions. A game without it gets "The game moved on while you were away."
  or "You didn't miss anything." plus the seconds left.
- Strings in `i18n-en-phone.ts` / `i18n-es-phone.ts` (`offline.*`), Spanish throughout.

## Proof (probe `offline-791.tmp.ts`, port 42221; iPhone 15 VIP, iPhone SE, Pixel 7, Pixel 200 %)

Lightning question 1, Priya (Pixel) answers, then all four phones go offline (`setOffline`):

- 2.6 s later every phone shows the offline card; `elementFromPoint` at 70 % height hits the veil or
  the card on all four. Pixel: "✓ Your answer was sent"; the other three: "You haven't answered
  yet…". Seat held 1:59 / 1:58. A tap on the SE's answer A during the outage did not register
  (on return the SE was told it missed questions 1 and 2).
- The VIP skipped to question 3 while they were away. On return every phone showed "You're back"
  for ~1.0 s (iPhone 849–1905 ms after the link returned, SE 1700–2768, Pixel 916–1907, 200 %
  4141–5156): iPhone/SE/200 % "You missed questions 1 and 2. This is question 3 of 10: 8–12
  seconds left."; Pixel (answered Q1) "You missed question 2." Then question 3, live.
- 173 frames per phone at ~10 fps: dissolve ghosts existed on 4–5 frames per phone (the catch-up
  swap) and were visible on **0**.
- Spanish run: "Reconectando…", "Tu teléfono perdió el Wi‑Fi de la fiesta. Tu lugar está guardado
  durante 1:59.", "Aún no respondiste: podrás hacerlo al volver", "Ya estás de vuelta", "Te
  perdiste las preguntas 1 y 2. Esta es la pregunta 3 de 10: quedan 11 segundos.", "Tú · 1.º · 0".
