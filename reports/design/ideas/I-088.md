# I-088 · The room's own size, in the ★ menu (option C)

Branch `b/I-088-C` reapplied (`VipMenu.tsx` and `protocol.ts` conflicted against the owner's
public/private button and I-077/I-082's neighbours; `engine/vip.ts` and the menu's CSS applied
clean).

## What ships

- **A** — the VIP action `setCapacity` (4–16, clamped and never below the head count) and a
  "Room size − X / N +" row in the ★ menu; the TV's "X / N players" follows.
- **B** — the − greys out at the floor (the head count, or 4).
- **C** — "Lock at this size (N)": one tap sets the capacity to the head count and locks the room;
  Unlock undoes it, and the button hides while the room is locked.

## Proof

same time:


The stepper moves the room and the TV together, both ends stop, and one tap sets the size to the
head count (floored at 4) and locks the room. `pnpm verify` GREEN.
