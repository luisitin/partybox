# I-790 C (design review) — the reveal happens on the answers, and the result is one line on top

The owner approved proposal C of the design review (f4f43d17); its drawn mock-up (iPhone SE) is
the target. No Session B branch: built on top of I-789 B.

## What ships

- **The tiles carry the verdict.** The right answer: green ring, filled disc, "✓ the answer"
  (or "✓ you" when it was your pick); a wrong pick: red ring and "you"; the rest dim. A 2×2 tile
  (its mark is a corner badge) keeps the glyph only — ✓ on the answer, ✗ on a wrong pick — so it
  never relies on colour. SDK `ChoiceGrid` strings "the answer" / "you" (Spanish: la respuesta, tú).
- **One band under the question** (new `band` slot on `ChoiceGrid`, between the prompt and the
  answers): the verdict (Correct! / Wrong / Too slow / No answer, + 🔥n on a streak or "Streak of n
  over"), then the two numbers apart — "+0 this round" in the verdict's colour and "987 total"
  muted at the right edge. The old card under the list (and "It was B · …", "1000 points") is gone.
  It rises a beat after the marks (pb-rise, fast-token delay, like the card did); it takes the place
  of the held "+N now" line, so the tiles barely move at the reveal.
- The VIP's Next button stays the reveal's footer; a non-VIP phone has no footer now (it used to
  hold the card).
- Strings: "{delta} this round" / "{total} total" with Spanish; the old card's six sentences were
  removed from the Spanish table.
