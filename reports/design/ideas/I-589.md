# I-589 B — the reveal shows the race, then the standings (+ the owner's Next button)

Shipped from `b/I-589-B` (cherry-picked clean) plus the owner's binding note on the pick:

> Maybe a bit more time at the standings page with a "next question" button the vip or tv can
> click to go next. If a new button is added then the "skip/next" should be hidden in this game.

## What ships

- **A** — a regular reveal deals the right answers fastest first, each with its time, ⚡ on the
  fastest (`rows[].elapsedMs`, server sort).
- **B** — 2 s in (`RACE_MS`, client/timing.ts) the rows re-deal into score order; the times go.
- **The note** — a regular reveal now holds **8 s** (was 5: the standings get 6 s instead of 3);
  the final reveal keeps 5 s. With the standings a **Next question ▶** pill (**On to the wager ▶**
  after the last regular question) arrives on the TV, in the header's topic slot, and a
  **Next question** button under the VIP's outcome card. Both send the engine's VIP skip; one tap
  locks the button. The view carries `next` and the envelope's new optional `vipSkipHidden`, so
  the TV host bar and the ★ menu leave out their generic Skip / Next **in that phase only**.
- Shell plumbing (additive): `ViewEnvelope.vipSkipHidden?`, `GameTvProps.skip?` (TvApp → TvPlaying
  → the game's Tv; the TV is the screen the party is run from, ADR-031). TvApp's stage switch moved
  to `tv/tvContent.tsx` to stay under the 300-line cap.
- Manifest pace: `perRoundSeconds` 16 → 19 (the 3 s longer reveal).
