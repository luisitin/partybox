- **A** — the TV roster chip's ▲ is a small "1st" tag in a ring of its own colour ("1.º" in Spanish,
  aria "leading" / "va ganando"): `LeadMark` in `@partybox/game-sdk/ui`, drawn by `PlayerChip leader`.
