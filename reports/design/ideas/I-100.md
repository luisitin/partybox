# I-100 · Bingo · large text never clips the card — shipped option C

Cherry-picked `b/I-100-C` (b5c542b2) cleanly:

- **A** `.roundBody` scrolls (`overflow-y: auto`) — the whole grid is reachable under the sticky
  footer at any scale.
- **B** the pattern hint clamps to two lines.
- **C** the phone's six type tokens are rem (`1.125rem` = 18 px at a 16 px root), so a larger-text
  setting scales the words; the TV column stays px. Noted in docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md's type scale.

Two fixes on top, both found by the probe at 200 % and both only visible once C lets the words grow:

- The card-pick footer (`.introActions`) is `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(min(100%, 7rem), 1fr))`: side by
  side at normal sizes, stacked at 200 % — the plain `1fr 1fr` pushed **Ready** off the right edge
  of a 320 px phone ("Rea").
- `.introCount` is `flex-shrink: 0`: in the scrolling body the two-line "everyone is waiting for
  you" was squeezed to its 28 px min-height and its second line drew over the B I N G O letters.
