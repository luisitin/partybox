# I-141 · Blanks · the hand as the owner designed it — shipped design B (not branch C)

Session B's note overrides the branch: the owner picked **design B** on the fan options page
(options/design/I-141-fan.html), so `b/I-141-C`'s thin row was not applied. Built from the mock-up:

- **The fan** (`HandFan.tsx` `useFan`): each card shaped from its distance d to the centre, in cards,
  on every scroll frame — rotate(d × 14°) about its bottom centre (the sides' tops point away), the
  middle card lifted 18 px, the sides dropped 26 px a card and 12 % smaller, z by closeness. Cards
  are a fixed width (`min(260px, 62vw)`) and the row pads by half the rest, so the first card and
  the New hand card can snap to the centre and stand upright.
- **The counter**: page dots with "3 of 10", the current dot 22 px and gold, the New hand dot a
  hollow dotted ring (a dotted gold pill when there); hung from the pinned black-card block into
  its own bottom padding — it takes no height.
- **New hand** is the fan's last card: "⟳ New hand · Swap all N cards for fresh ones · K left",
  dashed gold; a tap redraws (the existing input) and scrolls back to card 1; greyed at 0 left.
  The old "New hand · 3 left" text button is gone.
- **"Cards dealt"**, a new setting: 7 / 10 (default) / 12 / 15. The deal, the refill and the redraw
  follow it; half of any hand stays good (`goodFloor`: 7 → 4, 10 → 5, 15 → 8), and the fit target
  scales with it.
- Spanish for every new line and the setting.
