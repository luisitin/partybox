# Design recommendations — round 4, pass 2026-09-15-1930 (play-feel: motion, visual, sound, pacing)

Captured on `design` @ aa9c03f. Twelve finder passes (`ux-{core,wisecrack,lightning-round}-{motion,visual,sound-feel,pacing}/`: montages, bursts, slow-token captures and ad-hoc stills) covering every TV and phone screen of the core shell, Wisecrack and Lightning Round. No mechanics change is proposed: every item is client/game-sdk presentation, copy, cue or timing. 59 unified findings went through verification; 45 survive below, 14 are refuted at the end.

## Summary

1. **Root cause for most "hard cut" reports**: `pb-rise` never plays from any CSS module — postcss-modules hashes the animation name, so ten module-driven entrances (phase change, vote cards, reveal rows, toasts, sheets) are dead and only the global `.pb-enter` works (R-046). Fix once, plus a static guard test; several later items build on `global(pb-rise)`.
2. **Sound routing is dead on the TV and absent on the phone**: `clientModule.sounds` has no consumer, so every phase plays the same triad and the declared `reveal` sting has never been heard (R-047); the phone has no sound engine and never vibrates, so `submit`/`error` are aspirational (R-048).
3. **Input feel**: press states are invisible and lock-in waits for the server echo (R-049); the last five seconds on an unanswered phone are a 4 px bar (R-050); disabled buttons, rejected joins and game cards give no acknowledgement (R-078, R-088, R-090).
4. **Reveals spend their biggest beat in one frame**: Lightning's final-wager reveal and Wisecrack's vote reveal land fully formed, winner first, in generic rows (R-051, R-053, R-054); the correct answer is the smallest text on the stage (R-052); the phone spoils the TV by up to 1.5 s (R-059).
5. **Scoreboards**: overflow the stage at 5–6 and 13+ players, read ranks across not down (R-055), and land whole with no stagger or count-up (R-056); the strip never shows running scores (R-068).
6. **Phone screens are the only surface that hard-cuts** — every status change, prompt change and results screen (R-058, R-063, R-066); the reveal outcome card paints losses gold and clips at 200 % (R-065).
7. **Shell polish**: deadline bar refill sweep (R-069), curtain/scrim hard-coded to Night (R-071), paused phone looks like "answer now" (R-072), stray "…" on game start (R-073), lobby chips pop in with no entrance while the empty lobby never breathes (R-075), gate glyph mismatch (R-076), one envelope for every cue (R-077).
8. **Refuted (14)**: passive-phase alarm (ADR-030 `timerMode` already exists — hand-off to games), results ceremony and Lightning reveal choreography as written (both spoil via the phone), Daylight borders (would erase the winner outline), and ten smaller items that were stale, harness artifacts, or below the couch-visibility bar.

## Recommendation

Land R-046 first (S, unblocks R-057, R-062, R-066, R-069 and every `global(pb-rise)` reference below), then R-047 and R-048 (the two sound foundations; R-050, R-065, R-078 leave one-line call sites for the phone engine). The P1 reveal work (R-051 … R-054) and R-059 (phone hold) ship together so the phone never leads the TV. R-055 + R-056 are one Scoreboard commit. The P3 items are cosmetic and can wait for a quiet day. Owner picks; the refuted list at the end records what was rejected and why so the finders do not re-file it.
