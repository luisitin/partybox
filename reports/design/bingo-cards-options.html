Bingo Card Layouts

Options for playing with several cards on one phone, the FREE square on small cards, the "turn your phone" gate, and the card-picker idea. Every option has a letter — reply with your picks (for example "A1, B1, C-star, D1, E1, F-no") or tell me what to change. The blue-outlined ones are my recommendations. Cards below are drawings, not the real component, but the proportions are true to an iPhone 15.

A · Two cards your rule: landscape only

Two cards side by side in landscape. The question is what happens to the call header.

B · Three or four cards on a phone portrait or landscape

Each card fills its quadrant. Gaps tight, digits a notch smaller, no labels, no call header — the TV carries the call.

C · The FREE square on a small card

"FREE" does not fit a 30 px cell. Pick the mark. (It still reads as "FREE" to a screen reader.)

D · The "turn your phone" gate

Shown when the phone is held the wrong way for the card count: one card = upright only, two cards = sideways only, three or four = either. The gate lifts by itself the moment the phone turns, then asks the browser to lock the orientation (works on Android Chrome; iPhones ignore the lock, so the gate simply comes back if the phone is turned).

E · Tablets and computers

A wide screen is never asked to turn, and there is room for normal-sized cards.

F · Pick your card at the start one-card games

Your idea: instead of waiting through the intro, players with one card choose from up to four dealt cards. Cards chosen this way are still random — nobody sees the deck — so it is pure fun, not an edge.

G · Where the called-board toggle lives

On the game-selection screen, under Bingo's settings, next to "Previous number" and "Cheeky caller": Called board — off / on. It was never on the main branch before this week's merge, which is why it is not in your build yet; the snapshots you saw came from my branch. Default is now off, as you asked.
