Bingo Card Layouts

Version 5 — every decision so far, drawn; the theme buttons above repaint every mock-up with PartyBox's real palettes. Nothing here is coded yet beyond the baseline grid. Read top to bottom: the default ("Focus"), the card-style switcher and its pause, each style as you asked for it, then the confirmed picks. A few questions sit at the end.

1 · The default: Focus your B4, for one, two, three or four cards

One main card, big, with FREE spelled out and the call above it. Every card is a bordered thumbnail under it — the one that is up is greyed, tap another and it comes up in a quick slide (~150 ms), no shuffling of positions. Thumbnails are not daubable and use the ★. One card means no thumbnails. Portrait only.

2 · Changing card style a button next to the theme palette

Every phone has a "Card style" button in the top bar. Opening it pauses the calling only (a check or celebration already on the TV finishes first). Everyone else sees who is changing; that notice clears when the last sheet closes, then a 3 · 2 · 1 countdown and the caller resumes. Each phone keeps its own style and remembers it for the next game, like the theme.

3 · The other styles picked from the sheet

Each style says which way the phone must be held. Focus is portrait only; Side by side and Strip are landscape only; Stack and Grid are portrait only.

4 · Confirmed as drawn D1 gate · E1 tablets · F2 swap once

5 · Decisions so far

Topic Decision
Default style Focus: one big card with FREE and the call above it; every card as a bordered thumbnail below, the one up greyed; tap to swap, quick (~150 ms); thumbnails not daubable, ★ for FREE; portrait only. BINGO! checks the card that is up.
BINGO! checks Only the card that is up (the main card in Focus), never "your best card". The server sends the up card's index with the claim; if the wrong card is up, that is on the player. Open point for Grid / Strip / Stack / Side by side, where no card is "up": I propose the last card you tapped, marked with a gold border — say if you want each card to carry its own small BINGO! button instead.
Other styles Grid (portrait; "CARD n" → "BINGO ✓" + dim when won, card stays where it is; with 3 cards the spare slot is a mini call), Strip (landscape), Stack (portrait, no labels), Side by side (landscape, smaller cards, air above and below).
Style button In the top bar next to the theme palette. Per phone, remembered locally for the next game.
Pause Opening the sheet pauses the calling only; a running check or celebration finishes. Everyone sees "Sam is changing card style…"; the notice clears when the last sheet closes; then 3 · 2 · 1 with a tick each, and the next number.
Orientation Focus, Grid, Stack = portrait only; Strip, Side by side = landscape only. Wrong way = the D1 turning-phone gate; it lifts when the phone turns and asks the browser to lock (Android; iPhones ignore).
Tablets / computers E1: four normal cards in a row, labels on, no orientation rule.
Swap once (F2) One "Deal me another" per player per round during the 5 s intro; the old card is gone for good; no tap in time = no swap.
Called board Off by default; toggle in Bingo's settings at game selection.
