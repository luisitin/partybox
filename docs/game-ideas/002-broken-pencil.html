Broken Pencil

Telephone with a pencil: your word becomes a drawing, a guess, a drawing again — then every book is shown on the TV first page to last, one picture at a time, with the VIP turning the pages.

1The hook

Everyone knows the telephone game; this one has a pencil in it. You draw your secret word on your phone, the person next to you guesses what it is, the next person draws that, and by the time your book comes home a cat wearing a crown has become "a fat king". The laugh is the show at the end: the TV turns the pages of each book one at a time, first page to last, with the VIP holding the Next button and the room screaming at every turn. No points, no winner — the hamster is its own reward.

2A round from a player's seat

Ana is one of eight. Her phone offers three words — toaster, a cat wearing a crown, a pirate at the gym — plus a box to write her own. She taps the cat and locks it in; the TV shows "5 of 8 picked". Then her phone becomes a square sheet of paper with the word above it, eight ink colours, three pen sizes, undo, clear and a green ink bar. She draws a lumpy cat, a red nose, a yellow zigzag crown. The ink bar is at 40 %. She hits Done drawing; the phone shows her cat and says "Bo gets this next. Good luck, Bo." The TV shows eight cards with pencils, five ticked.

Next page: her phone shows a drawing she has never seen — Hal's, of what might be a lawn mower — and a text box. She types "angry vacuum" and sends. Then a drawing turn again: she is handed the words "haunted spoon" and has 60 seconds. The room is quiet except for giggling.

After the sixth page the TV says The show, and Ana's book is first. Every phone says Watch the TV; Gus, the VIP, has Next ▸ and Pause. The TV shows "a cat wearing a crown", then Ana's drawing (polite applause), then Bo's guess: "royal cat" — fine. Cy's drawing of a royal cat looks a lot like a potato. Dee's guess: "hamster king". The room loses it; Gus hits Pause so it can sink in. Eli's hamster king is a masterpiece. Fay's final guess: "a fat king" — CHAIN BROKEN in red, with the original word next to it. Gus taps Next and Bo's book begins. Seven more to go, and Ana is already crying laughing.

3Complete rules

Setup

  1. Seats: at start, players are shuffled into a ring of seats 0…N−1 (from state.rng). Every player owns one book.
  2. Pages: a book has L = 2·D + 1 pages: page 1 is the word, then drawing and guess pages alternate, ending with a guess. D is the number of drawings per book: min(settings.drawings, ⌊N/2⌋) (default 3 → 7 pages; 3 players → 1 drawing, 4–5 players → 2).
  3. Who writes what: page 1 (word) and page 2 (first drawing) are the owner's. Page p ≥ 3 of the book owned by seat b is done by seat (b + p − 2) mod N. Every player works exactly one page per step, and no player works the same book twice (guaranteed by the cap on D).

Play

  1. Pick (20 s): each player is offered three words from the pack (one easy, one medium, one hard, none repeated across players) and may instead type their own (1–30 characters, if customWords is on). Nothing picked by the deadline → the medium word is used.
  2. Draw (drawSeconds, default 60): draw the text on the previous page. Tools: 8 colours, 3 pen sizes, undo, clear. Ink is limited (≈1 100 simplified points per drawing); when it runs out the pen stops until you undo. "Done" sends the drawing; you may not edit after sending. No submission by the deadline → the page is an empty canvas.
  3. Guess (guessSeconds, default 30): look at the previous page's drawing and write what it is (1–40 characters). No submission → the page reads "???".
  4. Steps 5–6 repeat until every book has L pages. A step ends when every connected player has submitted or the deadline passes.
  5. Never spoil: while playing you only ever see the single previous page of the book in your hands. The TV shows only progress (who is done).

The show

  1. Books are shown in seat order, each from its first page to its last: the word, then each drawing (with its artist) and each guess (with its author) — one page on the screen at a time.
  2. The VIP turns the pages. The VIP's Skip (labelled "Next ▸" during the show) advances one page; Pause holds the current page as long as they like; Resume restarts its timer. Without a VIP action a page auto-turns after 6 s (word), 12 s (drawing) or 8 s (guess). Pages cannot be turned back (contract limitation, §13).
  3. After a book's last page the TV shows the final guess next to the original word and says UNBROKEN if they match (case-insensitive; punctuation and a leading "a/an/the" ignored) or CHAIN BROKEN otherwise. Then the next book, until all have been shown.

Scoring & winning

  1. There is no scoring. Nobody wins; the game is the show. results() reports 0 for everyone (all share rank 1) and one honorary Unbroken award per surviving book, to that book's owner.
  2. The final screen shows how many books survived and every book's first word beside its last guess.

Referee's notes

  • Disconnected players' pages are filled by the deadline rule (empty canvas / "???"); the book carries on. If the VIP is away the auto-turn timers carry the show.
  • Late joiners spectate the play phases and watch the show like everyone else.
  • Pause freezes the current step timer; drawing continues locally on phones but cannot be sent until resume.

6Data model

// games/broken-pencil/server/types.ts
import { z } from '@partybox/game-sdk';
import type { GameStateBase } from '@partybox/game-sdk';

export const PHASES = ['pick', 'draw', 'guess', 'show', 'done'] as const;

export interface Settings {
  drawings: number;      // drawings per book, 1..4, default 3 (capped at floor(N/2) in init)
  drawSeconds: number;   // 30..120 step 10, default 60
  guessSeconds: number;  // 15..60 step 5, default 30
  customWords: boolean;  // default true
  spicy: boolean;        // default false
}

/** One pen stroke. p = base64 of bytes [x0,y0,x1,y1,…], each 0..255 on a 256×256 canvas. */
export interface Stroke { c: number; w: number; p: string }   // c: colour 0..7, w: size 0..2
export interface Drawing { strokes: Stroke[] }

export type Page =
  | { kind: 'word';  authorId: string; text: string }
  | { kind: 'draw';  authorId: string; drawing: Drawing | null }   // null = not submitted (empty canvas)
  | { kind: 'guess'; authorId: string; text: string | null };      // null = not submitted ("???")

export interface Book { ownerId: string; pages: Page[] }

export interface State extends GameStateBase {
  settings: Settings;
  seats: string[];                       // seat index → playerId, shuffled at init
  pageCount: number;                     // L = 2·D + 1
  step: number;                          // play: index of the page being written (1..L−1); 0 during pick
  books: Book[];                         // books[b].ownerId === seats[b]; pages.length grows to L
  offers: Record<string, string[]>;      // playerId → the 3 offered words
  showing: { book: number; page: number; verdict: 'intact' | 'broken' | null; line: string | null } | null;
  intactBooks: number;
}

const stroke = z.object({
  c: z.number().int().min(0).max(7),
  w: z.number().int().min(0).max(2),
  p: z.string().regex(/^[A-Za-z0-9+/]+={0,2}$/).refine((s) => s.length % 4 === 0 && s.length >= 4),   // one point (a dot) is "AQI=": 3 chars + padding — do NOT require 4 data chars
});
export const INK_CHARS = 3000;                     // ≈ 1 125 points per drawing → ≤ 4.7 KB JSON
export const inputSchema = z.discriminatedUnion('type', [
  z.object({ type: z.literal('pick'), option: z.number().int().min(0).max(2) }),
  z.object({ type: z.literal('pickCustom'), text: z.string().trim().min(1).max(30) }),
  z.object({ type: z.literal('draw'), strokes: z.array(stroke).max(80).refine((a) => a.reduce((n, s) => n + s.p.length, 0) <= INK_CHARS) }),
  z.object({ type: z.literal('guess'), text: z.string().trim().min(1).max(40) }),
]);
export type Input = z.infer<typeof inputSchema>;
// No input exists during the show: page turns are VIP actions (skip = next, pause = hold).

export const PICK_MS = 20_000;
export const SHOW_MS = { word: 6_000, draw: 12_000, guess: 8_000 } as const;

Manifest: maxInputBytes: 6144 (ADR-002). A full drawing is ≤ 3 000 chars of points + ≤ 80 × ~20 bytes of stroke envelope ≈ 4.7 KB. State budget: 10 books × 4 drawings × 4.7 KB ≈ 190 KB worst case; 8 players × 3 drawings ≈ 115 KB. Under the 256 KB cap with margin — hence maxPlayers = 10 and drawings ≤ 4. Verified by the design simulation in §13.

7Reducer logic

Per-phase transition rules

PhaseEventRule
anyplayersetConnected; nothing else.
anyvip pause/resumeapplyVip (in show this is the "hold this page" control).
anyvip endenterDone; the summary is computed from every book's pages regardless of how far the show got. Books may be empty (VIP end during pick): views must render word: pages[0]?.text ?? "—" and isIntact must return false for books with fewer than 2 pages — the fuzz test in §13 hits exactly this.
pickinput pick / pickCustomPlayer only, once. option → offers[p][option]; pickCustom only if settings.customWords. Sets books[seat(p)].pages[0]. All connected picked → closePick.
picktimer · skipclosePick: unpicked players get offers[p][1]; step = 1; enterStep.
drawinput drawPlayer only, once per step, only if the book in their hands has exactly step pages. Append the page. All connected submitted → closeStep.
guessinput guessSame, appends a guess page.
draw/guesstimer · skipcloseStep: for every book with step pages, append the placeholder page (drawing: null / text: null) authored by the seat that owed it; step++; step < pageCount ? enterStep : enterShow(0).
showtimer · VIP skipturnPage: next page of the book; after a book's last page → first page of the next book; after the last book → enterDone. The verdict is computed when a last page is entered.
showany inputignored (no inputs exist in this phase).
doneanythingunchanged.

Pseudocode

// server/index.ts
function init(ctx) {
  const settings = readSettings(ctx.settings);
  const N = ctx.players.length;
  const D = Math.max(1, Math.min(settings.drawings, Math.floor(N / 2)));
  let rng = seedRng(ctx.seed);
  const [seats, r1] = shuffle(rng, ctx.players.map((p) => p.id).sort()); rng = r1;   // sorted first ⇒ deterministic
  const [offers, r2] = dealOffers(rng, seats, settings.spicy); rng = r2;                // 3 per player, no repeats
  const books = seats.map((id) => ({ ownerId: id, pages: [] }));
  const s = { ...base(ctx, rng), settings: { ...settings, drawings: D }, seats, pageCount: 2 * D + 1, step: 0, books, offers, showing: null, intactBooks: 0 };
  return enterPhase(s, 'pick', ctx.now, PICK_MS);
}

function reduce(state, event) {
  if (event.type === 'player') return setConnected(state, event);
  const vip = applyVip(state, event, {
    skip: (s, now) => s.phase.id === 'pick' ? closePick(s, now)
                    : s.phase.id === 'draw' || s.phase.id === 'guess' ? closeStep(s, now)
                    : s.phase.id === 'show' ? turnPage(s, now) : s,
    end: enterDone,
  });
  if (vip) return vip;
  if (state.phase.paused) return state;
  switch (state.phase.id) {
    case 'pick':  return reducePick(state, event);
    case 'draw':
    case 'guess': return reduceStep(state, event);
    case 'show':  return isTimerFor(state, event) ? turnPage(state, event.now) : state;
    default:      return state;
  }
}

/** Which book seat k holds at step i (0-based page index): the owner's own book for i ≤ 1 (word, first drawing);
 *  for i ≥ 2 the book b whose page i is authored by seat (b + i − 1) mod N — i.e. b = k − (i − 1).
 *  (An earlier draft had an off-by-one here; the design simulation caught it: at i = 2 the owner would have
 *  guessed their own drawing.) */
function bookInHands(s, playerId) {
  const N = s.seats.length; const k = s.seats.indexOf(playerId);
  if (k < 0) return -1;
  return s.step <= 1 ? k : (((k - (s.step - 1)) % N) + N) % N;
}
function enterStep(s, now) {
  const kind = s.step % 2 === 1 ? 'draw' : 'guess';
  const ms = (kind === 'draw' ? s.settings.drawSeconds : s.settings.guessSeconds) * 1000;
  return enterPhase(s, kind, now, ms);
}
function reduceStep(s, e) {
  if (e.type === 'input' && (e.input.type === 'draw' || e.input.type === 'guess')) {
    if (e.input.type !== s.phase.id) return s;
    const b = bookInHands(s, e.playerId); if (b < 0) return s;
    const book = s.books[b]; if (book.pages.length !== s.step) return s;                     // already submitted
    const page = e.input.type === 'draw' ? { kind: 'draw', authorId: e.playerId, drawing: { strokes: e.input.strokes } }
                                         : { kind: 'guess', authorId: e.playerId, text: e.input.text.trim() };
    const books = s.books.map((bk, i) => (i === b ? { ...bk, pages: [...bk.pages, page] } : bk));
    const next = { ...s, books };
    const submitted = next.books.filter((bk) => bk.pages.length > s.step).map((bk) => bk.pages[s.step].authorId);
    return allConnectedDone(next, submitted) ? closeStep(next, e.now) : next;
  }
  if (isTimerFor(s, e)) return closeStep(s, e.now);
  return s;
}
function closeStep(s, now) {
  const N = s.seats.length;
  const books = s.books.map((bk, b) => {
    if (bk.pages.length > s.step) return bk;
    const author = s.seats[s.step <= 1 ? b : (b + s.step - 1) % N];   // page index i ≥ 2 → seat b + i − 1 (rule 3)
    const page = s.step % 2 === 1 ? { kind: 'draw', authorId: author, drawing: null } : { kind: 'guess', authorId: author, text: null };
    return { ...bk, pages: [...bk.pages, page] };
  });
  const next = { ...s, books, step: s.step + 1 };
  return next.step < next.pageCount ? enterStep(next, now) : showPage(next, 0, 0, now);
}

// server/phases/show.ts — no inputs; VIP skip = turnPage, timer = turnPage, VIP pause = hold
function showPage(s, b, page, now) {
  const book = s.books[b]; const kind = book.pages[page].kind;
  let verdict = null, line = null, rng = s.rng, intactBooks = s.intactBooks;
  if (page === book.pages.length - 1) {                                   // last page: compute the verdict now
    const intact = isIntact(book);
    verdict = intact ? 'intact' : 'broken'; if (intact) intactBooks++;
    [line, rng] = pick(rng, intact ? LINES.intact : LINES.broken);
  }
  return enterPhase({ ...s, rng, intactBooks, showing: { book: b, page, verdict, line } }, 'show', now, SHOW_MS[kind]);
}
function turnPage(s, now) {                                                // timer AND VIP skip
  const { book, page } = s.showing;
  if (page + 1 < s.books[book].pages.length) return showPage(s, book, page + 1, now);
  return book + 1 < s.books.length ? showPage(s, book + 1, 0, now) : enterDone(s, now);
}
export const isIntact = (book) => { const last = book.pages[book.pages.length - 1]; return last.kind === 'guess' && last.text !== null && norm(last.text) === norm(book.pages[0].text); };
export const norm = (t) => t.toLowerCase().replace(/[^a-z0-9 ]+/g, ' ').replace(/\s+/g, ' ').trim().replace(/^(a|an|the) /, '');

// server/scoring.ts — no scores by design
export function results(state) {
  if (state.phase.id !== 'done') return null;
  const zero = Object.fromEntries(Object.keys(state.players).map((id) => [id, 0]));
  const awards = state.books.filter(isIntact).map((b) => ({ id: 'unbroken', title: 'Unbroken', description: '“' + b.pages[0].text + '” survived ' + state.seats.length + ' players', playerId: b.ownerId }));
  return buildResults(state, zero, awards);                                // everyone rank 1, winnerIds = everyone
}

Where randomness is drawn

  • init: one shuffle for seats; one shuffle per difficulty pool for offers (3 pools), then sequential dealing.
  • showPage on a book's last page: one pick for the verdict line.
  • Nothing else. Bots use their own rng argument.

sampleInput(state, playerId, rng) {
  const me = state.players[playerId];
  switch (state.phase.id) {

                    return { type: 'draw', strokes }; }

}

encodeWalk is a table-based base64 encoder over a random walk of quantised points — pure, no Buffer, no btoa. The client uses the same module to decode.

8Scoring

Decided by the owner (v0.2): no scoring. Broken Pencil is a show, not a contest. The contract still requires results(), so this section specifies the degenerate results exactly.
FieldValue
scores0 for every player from init.
rankingEvery player rank 1 (buildResults on all-zero scores does this).
winnerIdsEvery player. The engine's results screen will show everyone as winners; the game's own done screen (shown first) carries the real content: word → last guess per book.
awardsOne unbroken award per surviving book, to that book's owner: “toaster” survived 4 players. Zero awards when nothing survived.

Offers: with spicy on, the two packs are merged. Each player is offered one word of each difficulty; a game with 10 players needs 10 per difficulty, so each pool must hold ≥ 10 (family has 20 / 20 / 20).

Authoring guidelines

  • Difficulty 1: a single concrete noun anyone can sketch in 20 s. Difficulty 2: an action or a situation. Difficulty 3: a scene with two ideas that fight each other ("a pirate at the gym") — where the chains break.
  • Everything must be drawable without text; no brands, no living people, no puns that only work in one language.
  • Spicy = suggestive or embarrassing, PG-13, nothing explicit, nothing that targets a group.
  • Bot guesses are short nouns so replays read naturally.

12Accessibility & TV readability

  • TV sizes at 1080p: current drawing 560 px square (line widths 4 / 13 / 30 px, readable from 3 m); guess text 90 px; filmstrip rows ≥ 84 px with 32 px captions; verdict 48 px on a full-width banner.
  • Colour: verdict is a word plus colour; done/not-done pencil cards use ✓/✎ glyphs; ink bar has a text warning at 85 %. Drawing colours are the artist's choice — the paper is always light so any ink reads.
  • Screen readers: canvas is role="img" with aria-label="drawing canvas, {n} strokes"; toolbar buttons are labelled ("Colour pink", "Pen size large", "Undo last stroke", "Clear drawing"); the prompt card is aria-live="polite"; the show screen's live region announces each page ("Page 4, guess by Dee: hamster king"; "Page 5, drawing by Eli"). A drawing game cannot be fully non-visual — the guess phase and word/guess pages are; drawing pages fall back to "drawing by X".
  • Touch: colour dots 30 px on 41 px centres (≥ 44 px hit areas via padding); size buttons 40 px + spacing; canvas ignores touches that start on the toolbar; touch-action: none on the canvas so the page never scrolls mid-stroke; palm rejection by ignoring a second simultaneous pointer.
  • Reduced motion: no stroke replay, no page-turn slide, verdict without scale.
  • Time: drawSeconds up to 120 and guessSeconds up to 60; page auto-turn is the floor, the VIP can go faster (Next) or hold (Pause).

Design simulation (run before implementation)

docs/game-ideas/_tools/sim-002-broken-pencil.mjs is a headless model of this reducer (seats and routing, pick, draw/guess steps with placeholders, the VIP-driven show, results, both views) driven by contract-shaped events. Run: node docs/game-ideas/_tools/sim-002-broken-pencil.mjs. Results, 2026-09-15:

  • Routing: for every N in 3…10 and D in 1…4 (32 configurations) — every player writes exactly one page per step, no player touches a book twice, every book ends with a guess. The first run of this check found an off-by-one in the pseudocode's bookInHands (the owner would have guessed their own drawing at step 2); §7 is corrected.
  • Encoding: 500 random stroke round-trips pass; a one-point stroke (a dot) encodes to 3 chars + padding, which the first draft of the schema regex rejected — §6 is corrected. norm(): 5/5 cases.
  • Fuzz: 100 × 300 arbitrary events never threw — after fixing a second finding: tvView crashed when the VIP ended the game during pick (empty books). Views now tolerate books with 0–1 pages (§7).
  • Termination: 504 games (3–10 players; honest, fast, idle, max-ink and VIP-mashing strategies; defaults, both contract variants and the slowest settings) all reach done. Defaults: median 7.4 min, max 12.8 (bound 45). Slowest settings with an idle room: 26.7 min.
  • Never spoils: in every sampled view of every run, no TV view carried page text during play, no TV view carried pages beyond showing.page, no controller view carried any text page but the prompt and the player's own, and no controller view carried strokes during the show. 0 spoils.
  • Results: every run reports all-zero scores, everyone rank 1, one Unbroken award per intact book. Replays are byte-identical.
  • Sizes: largest state 185 KB with every drawing at the ink cap (10 players, D = 4) — under 256 KB with 28 % margin; typical games 40–90 KB. Largest view 19.3 KB (the show's filmstrip with four full drawings) — larger than the “a few KB” guideline; if the design review objects, send thumbnails (first 20 % of each stroke) for filmstrip pages.

SDK gaps (appended to sdk-requests.md)

  1. R-4 — a shared drawing primitive. BL-002 anticipates a drawing game; the canvas + toolbar + ink meter and the SVG renderer belong in @partybox/game-sdk/ui (DrawPad, DrawingView) with the encoding in @partybox/game-sdk. Build inside this game first, then lift.
  2. R-6 — VIP "back" and a page-friendly pause. The VIP can only skip (forward), pause, resume, end. Sliding backwards through the show needs a vip: 'back' action (contract change, ADR) or exposing the VIP id to controllerView. Also: the shell's pause curtain covers the stage; during a show the paused page should stay visible with a small ⏸ badge.
  3. R-7 — labelling VIP skip. The VIP overlay says "Skip"; here it means "Next page". A per-phase label from the game (view field vipSkipLabel) would make the control self-explanatory.
  4. R-2 (existing) — game-triggered sound cues for page turns and verdicts. R-1 (existing) — quiet timer for show pages.

14Open questions

  1. Decided (owner, v0.2): the VIP turns the pages; no scoring or voting. Implemented via VIP skip/pause because games never learn who the VIP is. Open: going backwards needs R-6 — recommended to ship forward-only first and add "Prev" when the contract allows it.
  2. Should everyone be allowed to press Next, not only the VIP? Recommended: no — one driver plus the auto-turn floor; a room full of Next buttons ends the show in a minute.
  3. Should the owner draw their own word first? Recommended: yes (Telestrations rule) — it guarantees the last page is a guess for every N and gives the owner skin in the game.
  4. Show the "next up" hint on phones? ("Your page is next.") Recommended: yes — it gives every player a reason to look up at the right moment without spoiling anything.
  5. Word pool size. 60 family words means a repeat-free game twice; recommended target 150 family / 40 spicy before release, same difficulty split.
  6. Ink budget. 3 000 chars (~1 125 points after simplification) fits a detailed 60-second doodle; if playtests hit the limit often, raise INK_CHARS to 4 000 and maxInputBytes to 8 192, and lower maxPlayers to 8 to keep state under 256 KB.
  7. Stroke replay on the show. Recommended: on (≤ 600 ms) — the cheapest spectacle in the game. Off under reduced motion.
