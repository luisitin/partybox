# SDK / contract requests from the design session

Things a design needed that `docs/GAME_CONTRACT.md` or `@partybox/game-sdk` cannot do yet. One entry per
gap, newest last; the owner decides what becomes an ADR or a backlog item. Status: open / accepted / done.

| #   | Requested by      | Gap                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Proposed shape                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Status |
| --- | ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| R-1 | 001 Bingo         | **Quiet timer mode.** Both shells' `Timer` turns red, scales 1.1× and plays `countdown` in the last 5 s of _every_ deadline. A phase that re-enters itself every 6 s (bingo calls) or lasts 5 s (a card check, a show page) would tick almost continuously.                                                     | Optional envelope field `timer?: 'normal' \| 'quiet' \| 'hidden'` (default `normal`) honoured by `TvPlaying` and the controller shell. Contract change → ADR. Interim heuristic: suppress urgency when the phase is shorter than 15 s (needs `startedAt` in the envelope).              | open   |
| R-2 | 001 Bingo         | **Game-triggered sound cues.** `GameClientModule.sounds` maps moments to cue names, but `GameTvProps` / `GameControllerProps` give a game no way to _play_ one; `TvPlaying` keeps `audio` to itself. Any in-phase moment (a new call, a page turn) has no sound.                                                | `useSound(): (moment: string) => void` exported from `@partybox/game-sdk/ui`, backed by a context both shells provide; resolves `moment` through `clientModule.sounds` and no-ops for unknown moments.                                                                                  | open   |
| R-4 | 002 Broken Pencil | **A shared drawing surface.** BL-002 expects a drawing game, but there is no canvas, toolbar, ink budget or stroke renderer in `@partybox/game-sdk/ui`, and no stroke encoding in `@partybox/game-sdk`. Every drawing game would re-implement ~500 lines of pointer capture, simplification and base64 packing. | `DrawPad` (pointer capture → quantised 256² points → RDP simplification → base64 strokes, undo, ink meter) + `DrawingView` (SVG renderer with optional replay) in `ui`; `encodeStrokes/decodeStrokes` in the pure surface. Build inside `games/broken-pencil` first, lift after review. | open   |
| R-6 | 002 Broken Pencil | **VIP "back" + a page-friendly pause.** The VIP can only skip (forward), pause, resume, end. The owner wants the VIP to slide through the show; forward works via skip, backwards is impossible today. Also the shell's pause curtain covers the stage, hiding the page the VIP wanted to hold.                 | Either a `vip: 'back'` action (contract change, ADR) or expose the VIP id in `controllerView` so a game can offer its own Prev/Next inputs. Pause: a `pauseStyle?: 'curtain' \| 'badge'` envelope field so a held page stays visible with a small ⏸.                                    | open   |
| R-7 | 002 Broken Pencil | **Labelling VIP skip per phase.** The VIP overlay says "Skip"; during the show it means "Next page", during a bingo check "Next number". Players hesitate to press a button called Skip.                                                                                                                        | Optional view field `vipSkipLabel?: string` rendered by the VIP overlay when present.                                                                                                                                                                                                   | open   |

Withdrawn: R-3 (penalty chip status) and R-5 (spectator inputs) — the mechanics that needed them were removed in the owner's simplification pass.
